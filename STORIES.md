## The 15 cards

1. **SHENZHEN · SUMMIT** — "Trump, Xi and Putin hold first three-way summit".
   90 minutes at Kylin Villa; a "Declaration on Peace": nuclear war "cannot
   be won and must never be fought". No mention of Ukraine, Iran, Taiwan or
   trade. First-ever trilateral of the three leaders; New START expired Feb
   2026. — Reuters / AP
2. **THE HAGUE · LAW** — "World Court: Myanmar committed genocide". By 9 votes
   to 6 the ICJ finds Myanmar guilty of genocide against the Rohingya in
   2017 — its first-ever state genocide finding. About 10,000 killed;
   700,000+ fled to Bangladesh. Reparations and return ordered; binding, no
   appeal. Case brought by The Gambia (2019). — Reuters / BBC / UN News
3. **ANTALYA · CLIMATE** — "COP31 draft drops fossil fuels and $120bn aid
   goal". The slimmed-down text, 80 paragraphs against 144 on Monday,
   deletes the fossil-fuel phase-out, net-zero dates and the $120bn
   adaptation target. It keeps the 35-by-35 electrification goal; talks are
   due to end Friday. A draft, not a decision. First COP text to acknowledge
   the 1.5°C overshoot. — Reuters / UN News
4. **CRIMEA · WAR** — "Ukraine hits Kerch rail line a third time". Kyiv says
   Neptune missiles hit the railway section overnight; Moscow halts trains,
   then restores one track by the afternoon. The road span fell on 17 Nov.
   Third strike in four days; Putin rejects further ceasefire. (Russia says
   drones — the Neptune claim is Kyiv's.) — Reuters / AP
5. **RIYADH · WAR** — "Houthi missiles shot down over Riyadh". The Saudi-led
   coalition intercepts missiles aimed at the capital; debris damages a
   tower in the financial district, no injuries. Four to five ballistic
   missiles to the south also intercepted; Saudi jets strike Sanaa. (Houthis
   claim they targeted the airport and financial centre — unverified.)
   — Reuters / AFP
6. **NEW YORK · MARKETS** — "Anthropic starts trading on Nasdaq". Priced at
   $83 on Wednesday, shares open at $111.50 and close at $97.17, up 17%.
   The $81.3bn raised is the biggest IPO on record, passing SpaceX's $75bn.
   Ticker ANT; 980m shares. — Nasdaq / AP
7. **WORLDWIDE · GAMES** — "GTA VI goes on sale after a 13-year wait".
   Unlocks at midnight in each country, New Zealand first; $79.99 on PS5
   and Xbox Series X|S, no PC version yet. Pre-load was 212 GB.
   — Rockstar / PlayStation
8. **ODISHA · STORM** — "Cyclone Akvan nears India's coast". Odisha evacuates
   72,000 people, with 200,000 listed to move, as severe cyclonic storm
   Akvan bears down. Landfall is due Friday noon near Gopalpur, winds
   90–100 kmph, gusting to 110. Red alerts in six districts; 74 rescue
   teams deployed. — IMD / ANI
9. **SEOUL · EDUCATION** — "Half a million sit Korea's big exam". 551,864
   candidates sit the Suneung at 1,312 centres; flights pause for 35
   minutes for the listening test. Emergency phone alerts catch 22
   candidates with phones hidden; 98 rule violations in all.
   — Yonhap / AJP
10. **CANBERRA · ECONOMY** — "Australia's jobless rate hits 4.9%". The
    highest since October 2021, as employment falls 43,500 in October. The
    Reserve Bank's cash rate sits at 4.85% after five hikes this year;
    markets now price a cut by mid-2027. September was 4.7%; the ASX 200
    fell 1.04% to 8,730.4 on the day. — ABS / Reuters
11. **JAKARTA · LAW** — "Indonesia passes asset-seizure law after 18 years".
    Parliament passes the bill letting the state seize assets worth Rp1bn
    or more without a criminal conviction, for corruption, narcotics,
    terror, trafficking and IT fraud including crypto and online gambling.
    In force in 12 months; not retroactive; an independent Asset
    Management Body will hold seized assets. — AP
12. **GAZA · WAR** — "Israel ends first raid past Gaza's Yellow Line". The
    army says its ground operation in central Gaza is complete and reopens
    the Salah al-Din road. Hamas calls the raid, the first publicly
    announced crossing since the October 2025 truce, a flagrant breach.
    The raid began Wednesday 18 Nov; Thursday's news is that it ENDED.
    Target was a tunnel west of the line; medics say three people were
    killed. — Reuters / AFP
13. **BOGOTÁ · LABOUR** — "Colombia's unions hold a national strike".
    Teachers and workers march in Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, Barranquilla and
    Bucaramanga against President De la Espriella's Economic Rescue Law.
    Called by the CUT union confederation and the Fecode teachers' union.
    — Reuters
14. **WASHINGTON · IRAN WAR** — "Trump: no Iran decision until next year".
    "I'll talk to you about it after the beginning of the year," Trump says
    aboard Air Force One. "We've got time." He weighs new strikes — the NYT
    reported Wednesday that Netanyahu pushes a 4–7 day air campaign — but
    says he is not rushing. Iran readies a tanker run at the US blockade
    from Kharg Island. — Reuters / Fox / Axios
15. **GREENLAND · SECURITY** — "Denmark backs Arctic pact with US, 109–0".
    Parliament approves the Greenland defence agreement, signed in
    September, by 109 votes to none. Motion B 36. — Folketing / Reuters
